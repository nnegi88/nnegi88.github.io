Case Closed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-12

Who we are

Case Closed is an idle detective game published by Naveen Negi (contact: nnegi88@gmail.com).

Data we collect

The game itself stores your progress locally on your device. We do not require an account and never collect your name, email, or contacts.

We use the following third-party services, which may collect data as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may process your advertising ID and coarse device data to serve and measure ads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — anonymous gameplay events (e.g. which upgrades are bought) and crash reports, used to improve the game.

Ads and consent

All ads in Case Closed are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent prompt is shown before personalized ads are served.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes your local save. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, contact us at the address above.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change.
